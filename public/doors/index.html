Weekly Brief

Briefing 18

2 October 2026 · one sitting

One edition. Read it down the page: what changed, why it matters, then the two things you can try.

Trust · 01

FTC opens a probe into OpenAI and Anthropic

Changed. On 30 September the FTC confirmed an industry-wide look at rogue AI agents, with demands and executive testimony on the table.

Matters. “We signed the White House accord” is no longer a sufficient answer in a vendor review.

Rules · 02

Hawley previews liability for rogue agents

Changed. A Senate chair is drafting rules that would hold firms for reckless design and users for reckless deployment.

Matters. Insurance and procurement should price statutory liability, not only a contract indemnity.

Money · 03

SoftBank closes its final $10bn tranche

Changed. Cumulative OpenAI investment is now $64.6bn, about a 13% stake. Valuation and IPO timing stay unpublished.

Matters. A named shareholder finished the follow-on. That is a fact. It is not a price signal for your SKU.

Try · 04

Gemini 4 Argon, gated to cyber defenders

Changed. Google’s new frontier model starts in the Fairwind program at $2 / $10 per million tokens, with a 1M output limit.

Matters. Do not score it from the announcement. Ask whether you can get in, and keep it out of production until that is clear.

Try · 05

OpenAI ships always-on Dots

Changed. Persistent agents with their own computer are on Pro and Business Premium in eligible markets. Enterprise stays off until an admin turns it on.

Matters. You govern permissions and review gates, not every keystroke. Pilot one low-risk loop.
